## Specification: application rules adopted by the team from review round 4, §3 (`docs/peer_review_round4_nasa.md`)
Rules R1–R13 and the texts of §3.3 were proposed by the round-4 SI review (Claude, isolated session) and adopted by the team after reading it; as adopted, they are the spec. Summary: snap the drawn area to whole 0.25° cells and show the count; four tiers by cell count (≥ 100 full; 25–99 full with "reconstructed" label pre-2012; 4–24 baseline 2012–2025 by default, pre-2012 zero = "no MODIS detection"; 1–3 pre-2012 greyed and excluded unless toggled); interval from `cells.json` → `band_by_region_size[set][region][size_class]` (class by the number of cells inside the AOI; region = region of the cells carrying the largest share of H; whole country → `band_by_region_class[set].national.agg`; clamp p10 ≤ 0 ≤ p90; set `T` before 2002-07, `TA` to 2011-12, none for observed months; widen by |0.025·ONI| per side pre-2012); Terra-only era (Nov 2000 – Jun 2002) only for ≥ 100 cells, hatched; Tierra del Fuego from 2012 only; Chubut and Santa Cruz one tier stricter; static sources never added to H, shown as a separate series from 2012 with a banner when > 10 % of the area's detections; cloud-flagged months with a marker, no percentage; gap-filled months marked, share shown when > 10 %; `factor_source = assigned` labelled "factor borrowed from the region"; 2026 = near-real-time through the bridge (g = 0.979 NOAA-20, 0.951 NOAA-21 → S-NPP-equivalent), labelled preliminary, month to date; CSV download with unit, sensor flag, interval, gap-fill, cloud, factor source, tier and baseline used.

## Data the app reads
- `data/harmonized/cells.json.gz` (1.15 MB): `grid`, `years` 2000–2025, `cells[id] = { p: province, h: [26][12] harmonized counts (−1 = no data), s: [26][12] 1 = reconstructed/gap-filled, st: static counts by year, gf: gap fill, cl: cloud anomaly ×100 }`, `band_by_region_size`, `band_by_region_class`, `region_of_prov`, `note`. To publish: R2 bucket `geofuego-data` (exists), or Pages asset.
- Live 2026 — **the app is fully independent of geofuego.com.ar (decision taken in the plan; no D1, no Worker, no shared infrastructure).** Source: the FIRMS Area API, `VIIRS_{SNPP,NOAA20,NOAA21}_SP` for 2026-01-01 → 2026-07-31 and `_NRT` for 2026-08-01 → today (S-NPP NRT ends 2026-11-02), per point, run through the same grid → static mask (`data/agg/static_cells.json`) → bridge (`data/factors/bridge.json`) as the archive and shipped as a per-cell preliminary payload (`cells_2026.json`, month to date, data date stamped), regenerated by script before each deploy. The GeoFuego D1 export in `data/validation/` is a one-off cross-check of the bridge (0.982 vs 0.979), not an input.
- Province polygons `lib/provincias.js`; cell geometry from `lib/geo.js` (GRID latMin −55, latMax −21, lonMin −74, lonMax −53, step 0.25, 136 × 84).

## Decisions already taken (team)
Nothing automated; every deploy/publish/submit with explicit OK. Scope = exactly the challenge: AOI, date range, 26-year calendar with interval and flags, this month vs median (with the tier rules), CSV/PNG download, static-source layer. No FWI, no GFI, no forecasts. Vendored libs, CSP unchanged, noindex until the repository and the Zenodo record (10.5281/zenodo.24220107) are published, before the submission closes on Sunday 23:59. As delivered: a 30-second demo video and a three-minute local presentation, both introduced by an SI-generated presenter and voice with a visible label (declared in the README and on the About page); every screen after the introduction is the real app.

## Claims policy (round 4 §4)
Allowed: reproducible series, out-of-sample errors 3/10/30 %, 2024–25 national within 2–5 %, the post-2011 fall is real, the naive splice is not. Softened: "sensor of origin no longer matters" only for ≥ ~100 cells; pre-2012 error conditional on stationarity. Forbidden: validated before 2012, fires/burned area, NASA product or alert system, zero pre-2012 = no fire, cloud corrected, transferable to other countries, 2026 in the record.
