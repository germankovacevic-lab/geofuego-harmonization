# Data audit, 14 Nov 2026 (noon) — what we checked, what changed, what it did to the numbers

Context: after the engine was complete (v1, 10:32) we stopped and audited data, method and conclusions before building the app. Three checks found real issues; all fixes are in the scripts and every number below is reproducible.

## Findings

1. **Orbit drift of Terra and Aqua.** Terra's equator-crossing time drifts earlier since 2020 and Aqua's later since 2022. Measured nationally (vegetation class): Aqua/Terra 1.7–1.9 in 2003–2023 → **2.23 (2024), 2.46 (2025)**; VIIRS/Terra ~9 → **11.6 (2025)**. Factors fitted with 2024–2025 would be applied to 2000–2011 when both satellites were nominal. → **Calibration window 2012–2023** (`04_factors.mjs`, `05_loyo.mjs`). Effect on the Terra-only factor: −1.5 % on average.
2. **Satellite outage days inside the series** (`03b_coverage.mjs`; outage = zero national detections while the count expected from another satellite that day is ≥ 8, or a ≥ 3-day zero-run framed by ≥ 5/day): Terra 180 days (Jun 2001: 15, Oct 2022: 13, Mar 2002: 9), Aqua 88 (Apr 2022: 16, Aug 2002: 7), S-NPP 38 (**Aug 2022: 10 — safe-hold in the peak month of the decade's largest fire year**, Jul 2024: 11). Two consequences:
   - **Calibration on paired days only** (Terra, Aqua and S-NPP all operating): columns `Vp,Tp,Ap` in `counts.json`.
   - **Cross-sensor gap filling in the harmonized series** (`06_harmonize.mjs`): VIIRS outage days are filled with f·MODIS of those exact days; Terra/Aqua outage days in 2002–2011 are filled from the other MODIS satellite with the province×month T/A ratio; Terra-only months (2000–mid 2002) are scaled by days. Every filled value carries `gap_fill` and the outage-day counts. Added VIIRS-equivalent hotspots: S-NPP outages 13 243 (2022: +5 635, 2024: +7 086), Terra 5 871, Aqua 5 723, Terra-only era 10 294 (Jun 2001).
3. **A processing bug caught by the re-run:** after downloading the VIIRS SP files for the NOAA-20/21 bridge, `03_aggregate` and `03a_static_sources` iterated every file in the manifest and double-counted VIIRS 2023–2025. Fixed by filtering on sensor. (The committed v1 results predate those downloads and were not affected; the bug would have surfaced on the next run.)

## Still declared, not solved
- Provinces with little fire (Santa Cruz, Tierra del Fuego, CABA) inherit the country factor (w ≈ 0.6 or 0) and carry a wide band; their burned-area-per-hotspot is erratic even in observed years (r ≈ 0.3), so no method can be validated there.
- Single-cell monthly values: ±36 % (LOYO). The band says so.
- Unit is VIIRS-equivalent hotspot counts (375 m pixels), not burned area nor number of fires.
- Gap-filled values in observed years keep σ = 0 in the band (the filled share is small, < 3 % of a year); flagged in the CSV.
